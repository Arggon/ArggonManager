# Role model: agents as primary workers, humans as product owner (invariants, promotion policy, recorded acceptance)

## Context

The sentence saying everyone follows the same rules is only half true, and this
epic records the honest half. Anyone or any helper may pick up work, produce
it, review it, comment on it and merge it under the same rules and the same
records — that half holds. What the sentence hides is that a small set of calls
belongs to the product owner, and the irreversible ones only a person can take.
Those calls were real but unwritten, and the most important one — whether a
finished piece of work is accepted — left no trace at all, while the day-to-day
work had quietly become helper-led without saying so.

This epic declares the split: the loop stays identical for everyone, authority
is named, and acceptance gets a durable home. Its single story, the role-model
foundation, carries the exploration, the records, the role contracts and the
carrier wiring that make the split stick. (ADR 0021; ADR 0026.)

## Acceptance

Complete when the role-model-foundation story is terminal and the product owner
has recorded acceptance on this epic. Nothing further is checked at this level.
