# Transfer research agent-harness results into the methodology: AutoHarness, AutoContext, AutoCompact

## Context

The evidence behind the earlier decline turned out to describe registry packages
that merely share the papers' names; the product owner supplied the actual
research — three papers, not a vendor stack. A research finding proposes no new
executor, so adopting one runs inside the existing loop under the existing
roles, and the decline's decision stands while its evidence base is superseded.

This story transfers what the papers actually change into the method: the
compaction diagnostic, the exploration stopping rule, and measures of how often
the shared kernel refuses an attempt — each on the existing substrate, none as
a second loop. (ADR 0022 amendment; exploration-harness-research-transfer-021.)
